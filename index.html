Verification · step 3

Stock & harvest check

Stock movements must balance for the same period, and each farm's production must be plausible for its harvested area and the local yield.

Harvest 2025/26 · Feb–Jul 2026

Stock check · Coop. Oeste Baiano, Luís Eduardo Magalhães

Opening stock + Production received + Purchases = Deliveries + Closing stock + Losses

In plain words: everything that came into the cooperative must equal everything that left, plus what is still in storage, plus normal losses (drying, dust). If more left than came in, some soy has no recorded source.

≠

Harvest check: is the yield believable?

Reference yield 3.80 t/ha for western Bahia 2025/26, tolerance ±15%. A live system takes the reference from CONAB municipal estimates. Figures come from the producer portal.

Storage bins

About
How it works
One approval process that links land checks and amount checks, for direct and indirect suppliers, before soy is mixed.

  

  
What changes in the three supply chain flows

    

      
Product flow
Deliveries waiting for checks stay in separate holding bins. Only approved soy is mixed for EU-bound crushing.

      
Information flow
Farm IDs are linked to contracts, truck tickets and truck scale records. Amounts sent are compared with amounts received.

      
Finance flow
The final payment is released only after checks are complete, under agreed contract terms.
